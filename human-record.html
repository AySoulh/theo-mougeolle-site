Human Record

Role

Art direction

Scope

Merch, vinyl, motion

Focus

Visual identity

Human Record is an identity built around a raw, organic visual world: a hand-drawn “HUMAN” lettering, applied across merch and a full vinyl packaging for the release of the record “Asquith”.

The logo’s irregular stroke appears on the t-shirts and hoodies, in two colorways (white and orange), then unfolds fully on the record sleeve: a fluid, saturated texture of blues, pinks and reds, wrapping the cover, the disc itself, the slipmat and the release poster.
